常见疾病症状

Symptoms of Common Diseases

内痔 Internal Hemorrhoids

症状 Symptoms

  • 无痛性间歇性便血 Painless, intermittent rectal bleeding
  • 痔核脱出 Prolapsed haemorrhoids
  • 肛门异物感 Sensation of a foreign object in the anus
  • 肛门潮湿与瘙痒 Anal Moisture and Itching

*便血也是直肠癌等恶性肿瘤的早期信号

*Blood in the stool can also be an early sign of malignant tumors such as rectal cancer

外痔 External Hemorrhoids

症状 Symptoms

  • 肛门异物感与肿胀 Sensation of a foreign object in the anus and swelling
  • 剧烈疼痛 Severe pain
  • 肛门发炎、肿痛与灼热感 Anal inflammation, swelling, pain, and a burning sensation
  • 肛门潮湿与瘙痒 Anal Moisture and Itching
  • 不易/少量出血 Rare/minor bleeding

混合痔 Mixed Hemorrhoids

症状 Symptoms

  • 便血与肿块脱出 Blood in the stool and prolapse
  • 剧烈疼痛 Severe pain
  • 严重的肛门潮湿、瘙痒与湿疹 Severe anal moisture, itching, and eczema
  • 肛门异物感与坠胀 Sensation of a foreign body and heaviness in the rectum

肛瘘/老鼠偷粪 Anal Fistula

症状 Symptoms

  • 肛周皮肤反复流脓、流水 Recurrent pus discharge and oozing from the perianal skin
  • 反复发作的肛门疼痛与肿胀 Recurrent anal pain and swelling
  • 肛周皮肤瘙痒、潮湿与湿疹 Itching, Moisture, and Eczema of the Perianal Skin
  • 可以摸到条索状的“硬结” You can feel a cord-like “lump”

肛裂 Anal Fissure

症状 Symptoms

  • 周期性肛门剧痛 Recurrent severe anal pain
  • 便血（血量通常较少） Blood in the stool (usually a small amount)
  • 便秘（因恐惧排便而加剧） Constipation (exacerbated by fear of defecation)
  • 肛门瘙痒与潮湿 Anal Itching and Moisture

肛周脓肿 Perianal Abscess

症状 Symptoms

  • 持续性剧烈胀痛与跳痛 Persistent, severe, throbbing pain
  • 肛周局部出现明显的红肿块 A noticeable red, swollen lump has appeared in the perianal area
  • 全身中毒性感染症状 Symptoms of systemic toxic infection
  • 肛门坠胀感与排便/排尿困难 A sensation of heaviness in the rectum and difficulty with bowel movements or urination

Our Advanced Treatments

中医挂线疗法

Seton Therapy

针对复杂性肛瘘与深部脓肿，通过慢性勒割与持续引流，在处理瘘管的同时促进肉芽组织生长，并尽量保护肛门括约肌。

Used for complex anal fistulas and deep abscesses, supporting gradual drainage and healing while minimizing sphincter damage.

微创镭射手术

FiLaC Laser Surgery

利用特定波长的激光能量清理坏死组织，并由内而外消融及闭合伤口或瘘管，减少创面、出血与疼痛感。

Laser energy helps clean, ablate, and close fistula tracts from within, with smaller wounds, less bleeding, and quicker recovery.

个性化药物治疗

Tailored Pharmacotherapy

配合局部消炎、镇痛药物，以及坐浴或熏洗方案，缓解急性症状、消除水肿并促进伤口愈合。

Personalized medication, sitz baths, and local care help relieve symptoms, reduce swelling, and support wound healing.
